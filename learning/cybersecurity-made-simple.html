THE BASICS

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, websites, networks, and digital information from unauthorized access or damage. That definition sounds technical, but the idea is actually very simple.

Think about your home. You lock your doors. You close your windows. You don't hand strangers the keys. Those simple habits help protect the things that matter most. Cybersecurity works in much the same way—except instead of protecting your house, it protects your digital life.

Every time you check your email, shop online, pay a bill, or log into your bank, you're trusting technology to keep your information secure. Cybersecurity is the combination of technology, processes, and good habits that helps make that possible.

WHY IT MATTERS

Cybersecurity Is Part of Everyday Life

Years ago, most important information was stored on paper. Today, much of our lives exist online.

We use technology to:

  • Shop online
  • Pay bills
  • Access banking
  • Book travel
  • Work remotely
  • Store family photos
  • Communicate with friends and family
  • Access healthcare and government services

Because so much information is digital, protecting it has become part of everyday life. Good cybersecurity helps reduce the risk of fraud, identity theft, financial loss, and unauthorized access to your personal information.

COMMON MYTH

"I'm Not Important Enough to Be Hacked."

Many people believe cybercriminals only target wealthy individuals or large companies. In reality, most cybercrime isn't personal.

Modern attacks are often automated. Criminals send millions of phishing emails, scan websites for weaknesses, and try stolen passwords against thousands of online accounts every day. They're not looking for one specific person—they're looking for anyone who makes a mistake.

Whether you shop online once a month or work in technology every day, you have information that's worth protecting.

WHAT CYBERSECURITY PROTECTS

The Four Things Cybersecurity Helps Protect

Good cybersecurity isn't just about computers—it's about protecting the parts of your digital life that matter most.

👤 Your Identity

Your name, address, passwords, and personal information.

💳 Your Money

Bank accounts, online purchases, payment cards, and financial transactions.

🔒 Your Privacy

Messages, photos, medical information, and personal documents.

💻 Your Devices

Computers, phones, tablets, and the accounts connected to them.
